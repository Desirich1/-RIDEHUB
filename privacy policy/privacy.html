Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with RIDEHUB.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Our commitment

RIDEHUB is built around trust. This policy explains the information we handle when you use our ride booking service and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, account information, pickup and destination details, booking history, and messages sent to our support team.

How we use information

Your information helps us provide rides, connect passengers with drivers, improve safety, process support requests, and communicate important service updates.

Sharing and security

We share only the information needed to coordinate a booking or provide a service. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data.

Your choices

You can review or update account details and contact us with questions about your information. Some data may need to be retained where required for safety, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact us
